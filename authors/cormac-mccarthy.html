Cormac McCarthy

Blood Meridian, or The Evening Redness in the West (1985)

Old Testament. I really enjoyed the Judge as a character, and the book definitely picked up once the fool was involved. It's bloody and gruesome, inane and insane, but clear about what matters to a person in this time. Honesty, integrity, character? Respect, will? There's no room for love, and no real connection, I'd say. They've bred a camaraderie, but only out of necessity and an inability to fully trust each other. I need to reread it.
