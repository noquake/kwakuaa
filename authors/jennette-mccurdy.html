Jennette McCurdy

Half His Age (2026)

It wasn't a direct recommendation, but I read this because Beth had recently finished it. It was a provoking book, at the very least. It's the most recent book I've read, and I can definitely see the difference between it and the older books I'd been reading before, in language, subject matter, and emotion in general. The links to consumerism, and the maladaptive coping it spurs on, were really interesting to read about this way.

With all that said, the main storyline, an underage girl's relationship with her teacher, was just a backdrop to her and her mother's own growth. That makes everything else, the explicit descriptions, the sex scenes, and so on, feel like noise. Above all else, Waldo's war against her own rage was epic in and of itself.
